# About Awesome Memes

Awesome Memes is a curated, continuously updated collection of memes — the popular ones everyone has seen and the niche ones only a corner of the internet knows. Every entry comes with a short explanation of its meaning, its origin and the humor behind it, and most include the image or template that defines them.

## What you'll find here

- **Classic and trending memes** — with the defining image or template where one exists.
- **Origins and meaning** — where each meme came from and how it's used.
- **Tags** — browse by format, era, platform or theme.

## How the collection is maintained

The collection is built and maintained with the [Ever Works](https://ever.works) platform. AI agents research memes, keep entries current and organize everything into categories and tags; each entry links back to its source.

Is a meme missing? Use the **Submit** page to suggest it.
